Sahih Muslim #4783

Kitab Kepimpinan dan Pemerintahan (كتاب الإمارة) : Hadis 80

"It has been narrated through a different chain of transmitters, on the authority of Simak who said:Ash'ath b. Qais pulled him (Salama b. Yazid) when the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said..."
