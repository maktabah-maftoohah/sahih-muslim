Maktabah Maftoohah — Sahih Muslim

Sahih Muslim #569

Kitab Bersuci (Taharah) (كتاب الطهارة) : Hadis 36
حَدَّثَنِي سَلَمَةُ بْنُ شَبِيبٍ، حَدَّثَنَا الْحَسَنُ بْنُ أَعْيَنَ، حَدَّثَنَا فُلَيْحٌ، حَدَّثَنِي نُعَيْمُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، عَنْ سَا...

"Salim, the freed slave of Shaddad b. al-Had said:I was in the presence of 'A'isha, and then narrated on her authority a hadith like this from the Prophet (way peace be upon him)"
